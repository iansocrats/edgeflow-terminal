# EdgeFlow Terminal — Official Polishing & Uniqueness Rules

Every dictated post on EdgeFlow Terminal for the Bybit ByX competition strictly adheres to the following 10 Approved Rules (3 Foundational Dictation Rules + 7 High-IMPACT Alpha Rules):

---

### Foundational Dictation Rules (Preserving 100% Spoken Voice)

1. **Rule 1 — Punctuation Only:**
   - Only missing periods, commas, colons, question marks, and capitalization of sentence beginnings are polished.
   - You are never allowed to rewrite whole sentences or change sentence structures.

2. **Rule 2 — Word Replacements Only When a Word Doesn't Seem Right:**
   - Word replacements are strictly limited to spoken numbers (e.g. `67800` ➔ `$67,800`, `15 minute` ➔ `15m`), transcription typos, or spoken abbreviations.
   - The user's authentic speech flow and vocabulary remain 100% intact.

3. **Rule 3 — Emojis, Spacing & Bullet Points:**
   - Suggestions for anchor emojis (e.g. `📊`, `📉`, `🎯`, `🛑`), paragraph line breaks for mobile readability, and bullet points (`•`) when listing levels or conditions.

---

### High-IMPACT Alpha Rules (Approved by Creator)

4. **Rule 4 — The "One Contrarian Angle" Rule (R1):**
   - Eliminate generic fence-sitting ("it could go either way"). Every post takes a clear, defensible position: *"The crowd sees X, but data shows Y."*

5. **Rule 5 — The "Skin-in-the-Game Invalidation" Rule (R2):**
   - Always define the exact invalidation price where your thesis is proven wrong. Builds creator trust and separates pros from noise.

6. **Rule 6 — The "Forbidden Consensus Phrase" Rule (R3):**
   - Strict ban on worn-out crypto clichés: *"could go either way"*, *"up or down"*, *"only time will tell"*, *"be cautious and be patient"*, *"do your own research"*.

7. **Rule 7 — The "Native Bybit Trade CTA" Rule (R4):**
   - Posts must include an organic conversion prompt to interact with the Bybit Trade Widget (driving the Valid Traders and Volume factors in the ByX IMPACT formula).

8. **Rule 8 — The "Single Metric Anchor" Rule (R5):**
   - Anchor the post around one striking, memorable metric (e.g., *"$1B spot outflow in 14 days absorbed at $79K"*), rather than listing a barrage of generic stats.

9. **Rule 9 — The "Hook Under 8 Words" Rule (R6):**
   - The opening line/title must be a scroll-stopping hook under 8 words (e.g. *"The $1B Absorption Paradox on Bitcoin"*).

10. **Rule 10 — The "Asymmetric Risk-Reward Frame" Rule (R7):**
    - Outline a clear risk-to-reward setup (e.g. risking 800 pts to catch a 3,200 pt move — 1:4 RR) to convert passive readers into active traders.
